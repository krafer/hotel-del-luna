Our Story

About Hotel del Luna

Six centuries of service to those who could not yet let go.

A House Outside Time

Hotel del Luna was founded in 1398, standing at the threshold between the world of the living and whatever comes after. To ordinary eyes, the building appears as a crumbling landmark — but to the weary traveler who has lost their way between worlds, it reveals itself in full: a grand hotel of red lacquer doors, gold-trimmed halls, and rooms that remember every guest who has ever stayed.

We do not simply offer a bed. We offer the space, and the time, to finish what was left unfinished.

Our Philosophy

Every guest arrives carrying something — grief, regret, unanswered questions. Our staff is trained not to hurry that away, but to sit with it, until the guest is ready to check out on their own terms.

A Brief History

Six Centuries, Selected Years

1398

The hotel is founded, hidden between the mortal world and the beyond, its doors first opened to a single grieving guest.

1592

The Celestial Suite is added to house nobility and generals lost in wartime, its lacquer bath said to wash away centuries of regret.

1897

The Moon Garden is planted, its blooms said to open only for guests who are close to finding peace.

Today

The house still stands, unseen by most, welcoming every soul who finds its door — including, perhaps, you.
